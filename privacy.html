Privacy notice

This notice explains, in plain language, how LinkPeople handles information you submit through this website.

Information we collect

When you use the employer enquiry form or the Talent Network form, we collect the details you provide — such as your name, contact details, company or professional information, your message or summary, and (for the Talent Network) a CV if you choose to upload one.

How we use it

Employer enquiry information is used to respond to companies and assess whether and how we may be able to help. Talent Network information and CVs are used to consider candidates for relevant current and future opportunities.

How submissions are delivered

Form submissions are delivered to private LinkPeople Google Workspace inboxes. Transactional emails (your submission and confirmation) are processed through our configured email service provider. In this version of the website, CVs are not intentionally stored in a public website database; a CV is attached to the internal notification email and is not published to a public URL.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We may share it with service providers only where required to operate the website and our communications. Candidate information may later be shared with a potential employer only in connection with a relevant opportunity and with appropriate communication or permission.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by emailing hello@linkpeople.agency.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect information. However, no method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Updates

This notice may be updated as the service evolves. Please check back for the current version.
