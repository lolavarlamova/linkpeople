About LinkPeople

Recruitment with context.

LinkPeople is a boutique recruitment and HR advisory partner for digital businesses. We combine focused market research, structured assessment and practical HR experience to help companies make stronger people decisions.

Our approach

We start with the business, not the job description. Every search begins with understanding the commercial goal, the team and the standard a hire needs to meet — so the shortlist reflects real relevance, not keyword matching.

How we evaluate fit

We assess candidates against the role, the team and the objective. Technology supports our research and sourcing; judgement about fit stays human and structured, with clear notes you can act on.

How confidentiality is handled

Many engagements are sensitive. We treat every search as confidential by default, protect candidate and client information, and share references and case details privately rather than publicly.

How the delivery model works

LinkPeople operates through a lean, senior-led model and scales delivery through trusted specialists when required. You work with experienced people throughout, and engagements are sized to what the search actually needs.
